# Viability

CohortLoom is not commercially proven yet.

That is the honest answer.

## What is already viable

- The local workflow is coherent.
- The UI and service layer are implemented.
- The synthetic demo path is clear.
- The Minds packet contract is strict.
- The app has a real audit trail and real safety gates.
- Live Minds persistence is verified for one synthetic approved hypothesis across three distinct
  conversations under the same Mind.

## What is not yet proven

- real creator adoption
- real retention
- real willingness to run 7-day experiments repeatedly
- real willingness to pay

## Commercial risk

The main risk is that the product could be useful as a thinking tool but still fail as a business.

That risk is real because the product and market evidence remains local and synthetic. The live
Minds proof verifies transport and continuity, not product demand.

## What would make it viable

The minimum credible path is:

1. show repeated usage by a real creator
2. show that the weekly hypothesis loop changes behavior
3. show that the product is used again after the first experiment
4. show some form of external value, not just local completion

## Practical read

If the goal is a hackathon submission, CohortLoom is viable as a demo and a product concept.

If the goal is a real business claim, it is not proven yet.

That distinction should stay explicit in every external write-up.
