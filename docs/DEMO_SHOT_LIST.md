# Demo Shot List

Target runtime: 90 to 120 seconds.

| Time | Shot | On-screen focus | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-0:08 | Title card | CohortLoom name and loop summary | Set the product in one sentence. |
| 0:08-0:18 | Dashboard load | Synthetic weekly metrics | Show that the input is weekly signal, not inbox data. |
| 0:18-0:30 | Hypothesis form | One falsifiable audience hypothesis | Show the creator-approved decision point. |
| 0:30-0:42 | Approval gate | Approve / reject buttons | Show that the app does not proceed without human approval. |
| 0:42-0:56 | Experiment panel | 7-day manual plan, success, stop conditions | Show the bounded experiment loop. |
| 0:56-1:10 | Minds section | Store packet / recall packet / due-review packet | Show the persistence layer and explicit send boundary. |
| 1:10-1:22 | Audit trail | Local audit log entries | Show that the app records state changes. |
| 1:22-1:34 | Safety states | Pause, no auto-post, no outreach | Reinforce the hard exclusions. |
| 1:34-1:45 | Closing frame | Weekly metrics -> hypothesis -> experiment -> due review | End on the product loop. |

## Editing notes

- Keep the pace calm and readable.
- Use short captions, not dense paragraphs.
- Make the synthetic status visible at least once.
- Keep real users, real revenue, and measured growth at zero; show only the verified live
  continuity label backed by the private redacted evidence artifact.
