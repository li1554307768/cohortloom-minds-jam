## One-line summary

Persistent experiment memory for creators.

## Short description

CohortLoom turns weekly engagement metrics into one creator-approved audience hypothesis, one bounded 7-day manual experiment, explicit success and stop conditions, and a due review.

It is local-first, audit-friendly, and deliberately narrow. It is not an inbox, a content-idea generator, a correction tool, a social scheduler, or an outreach tool.

## Problem

Creators often have data, opinions, and scattered follow-up ideas, but not a clean decision loop.

That makes it easy to lose the thread between:

- the weekly signal
- the hypothesis
- the experiment
- the due review

## Solution

CohortLoom keeps the loop in one place:

1. load a synthetic or local weekly summary
2. write one falsifiable hypothesis
3. approve or reject it
4. prepare a 7-day manual experiment
5. review the result later

## Why it is different

CohortLoom does not try to automate the creator.

It keeps the system small enough to inspect and strict enough to audit. The Minds layer is only for persistence and recall of approved decisions, not for hidden execution.
