# Competitive Differentiation

CohortLoom sits in the creator-workflow space, but it is deliberately narrower than adjacent tools.

The core promise is:

weekly metrics -> falsifiable creator-approved audience hypothesis -> 7-day manual experiment -> success/stop conditions -> due review.

That is a different job from collecting messages, generating ideas, patching content, or scheduling posts.

## What CohortLoom is not

- Not an inbox
- Not a content-idea generator
- Not a correction tool
- Not a social scheduler
- Not an outreach tool
- Not a hidden autopilot for publishing

## Comparison

| Product | Publicly visible center of gravity | How CohortLoom differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AudiencePulse.app | Audience signal reading, especially question or demand signals around content | CohortLoom does not stop at insight. It turns the weekly signal into one falsifiable hypothesis and one manual 7-day experiment with explicit stop conditions. |
| DealMinder | Creator revenue inbox and follow-up logic | CohortLoom is not triage for inbound deals or revenue opportunities. It works on audience experiments, not message handling, reply drafting, or deal follow-up. |
| ContextPatch | Patching stale context, correcting previously published material, and carrying changes across assets | CohortLoom does not patch published content. It records approved audience hypotheses and reviews the experiment loop; it does not rewrite the public record. |
| Reeti #48029 | Persistent creator continuity and memory across sessions | CohortLoom uses persistence, but it narrows the scope to one weekly hypothesis and one 7-day experiment. The due-review recall also omits the hypothesis body from the request itself. |

## Important nuance

DealMinder and ContextPatch are part of the same project lineage in this workspace, but the current submission should be read as CohortLoom, not as a generic rebrand.

The safest way to describe the difference is:

- DealMinder focused on creator revenue signals and inbox-like handling.
- ContextPatch focused on correcting stale public content and context.
- CohortLoom focuses on creator-approved audience learning and manual experiment review.

## Why this matters

The point of the hackathon entry is not to be another broad creator assistant.

The point is to make the decision loop specific enough that it can be:

- inspected
- approved
- replayed
- reviewed later

That narrow loop is CohortLoom's differentiator.
